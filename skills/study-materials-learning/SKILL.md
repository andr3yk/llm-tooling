# Study materials and palm cards (learning)

Execute the following steps chronologically when improving or expanding palm cards, flash cards, or study notes for a learner.

## Step 1: Preserve details and examples

1. Keep all concrete examples (e.g., SHA-1, MD5, RSA, Caesar cipher, 128 bits, 256 bits, algorithm names, key terms from the lesson).
2. Do not remove details or examples from the original material.

## Step 2: Improve language and clarity

1. Simplify the wording.
2. Use consistent terms (e.g., plaintext/ciphertext).
3. Add short clarifications only where they help. Do not replace or drop existing examples.

## Step 3: Format the cards

1. Format each palm card with one `##` heading.
2. Keep the same number of cards as the original. Do not combine or remove cards unless there is a documented reason.
3. Place bullets on the same line as the text (e.g., `• item one` not a bullet on its own line above the text).
4. Keep the flash-card style: concise bullets, scannable sections.

## Step 4: Document deprecations or combinations

1. If you merge or remove a palm card, add an explicit explanation in a blockquote at the top of the file or above the affected section (e.g., `> **Note:** Card X was combined with Y because …`).
